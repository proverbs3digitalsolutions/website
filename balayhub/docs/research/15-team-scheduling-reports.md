# Research 15: Team management, scheduling, and reports

Competitors covered: Guesty, Hostaway, Hospitable, Lodgify, Uplisting.
Research date: October 2026. Most vendor help pages were reachable only through search results, so several points are marked "unverified".

## 1. Summary

- This topic covers who can do what in the software, how cleaners and co-hosts work in it, how the team talks inside it, and what reports come out of it.
- Guesty is the deepest of the five. It has role-based permissions, a per-reservation activity log, team inbox assignment, internal notes, bulk calendar actions, and scheduled PDF emails of dashboards.
- Hospitable is strongest on teams. It has three user types, notification rules by team, internal notes with @mentions, and a separate Tasks App for cleaners.
- Hostaway has roles and user groups, tasks that can be assigned to a user, and a mobile app for tasks. Its report exports and scheduled emails are not confirmed.
- Lodgify and Uplisting are simpler. Lodgify's full analytics sit only on its top plan and are for the main account holder only. Uplisting has teammates and cleaners, a changeover flow for cleaners, and an emailed report.
- None of the five clearly offers shift or on-call scheduling. For small hosts this is a gap, and it is not a crowded market.
- For BalayHub, the closest wins are: co-host and cleaner logins with tight limits, a simple activity log, internal notes with @mentions, notification rules, and emailed PDF reports. All of these fit a host with 1 to 10 units.

## 4. Standout features

1. **Guesty per-reservation activity log.** Each change shows the user and time, and you can filter by team member. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19032452691101-Tracking-changes-made-to-a-reservation
2. **Hospitable notification rules by team.** You pick the event, the channel (email or SMS) and the property, and only people on that team get the alert. Source: https://help.hospitable.com/en/articles/4602296-notification-rules-how-to-create-a-notification-rule
3. **Hospitable internal notes with @mentions.** A mentioned person finds the thread under a Mentioned tab. Source: https://help.hospitable.com/en/articles/12311688-internal-notes-and-mentions-in-your-inbox
4. **Guesty bulk calendar actions.** Drag across many properties and dates to block or change rates in one go. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/34809775874845-Performing-bulk-actions-on-the-Multi-Calendar
5. **Uplisting changeover flow for cleaners.** The cleaner sees only changeovers. They accept, start and finish each one, and the owner is emailed when something is pending or changes. Source: https://support.uplisting.io/docs/adding-team-members-and-cleaning-teams
6. **Guesty scheduled dashboard emails.** Set a dashboard to email on a recurring schedule as PDF. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9370549610269-Advanced-Analytics-Exporting-and-automating-dashboard-reports
7. **Hospitable Tasks App with photo and checklist.** Cleaners work through checklists and upload photos from the phone browser. Source: https://help.hospitable.com/en/articles/13906293-overview-tasks-app
8. **Guesty owner statement approval.** Review and flag each statement, then send it to the owner portal or by email. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9370451622429
9. **Uplisting tags for groups.** Group listings into portfolios and view them together on the calendar. Source: https://support.uplisting.io/docs/tags
10. **Hostaway analytics by channel.** Revenue, bookings, ADR, occupancy and commission per channel. Source: https://www.hostaway.com/glossary/hostaway-analytics/

## 5. Complaints and gaps from reviews

- **Guesty app logs users out.** One reviewer reported this. Guesty said it is working on stability. Source: same.

## 6. Ideas for BalayHub

- **Must: Cleaner-only login with changeovers and checklists.** A cleaner should see only their own cleaning jobs, on a phone, with photos and checklist. This is the most-used team feature in every product here. Why: most Filipino hosts rely on one cleaner per building.
- **Must: Co-host login with per-property limits.** A co-host (like a relative or an assistant) should see only chosen units and only the calendar and messages. Why: many hosts share work with family.
- **Must: Activity log on each reservation.** Show who changed dates, price, guest count, status or notes, and when. Why: disputes over changed bookings are common and hard to settle without it.
- **Must: Internal notes in the inbox.** Team-only notes, shown differently from guest messages. Why: keeps handover details out of the guest's view.
- **Should: @mentions in notes with a Mentioned list.** Why: lets a manager pull in a cleaner or co-host without a separate chat.
- **Should: Notification rules by event and property.** Pick the event (new booking, cancellation, date change, check-in time change) and who gets it, by email or push. Why: staff get the alert they need, not every alert.
- **Should: Assign a conversation to a teammate.** One owner, one thread. Why: avoids two people answering the same guest.
- **Should: Scheduled PDF report by email.** Monthly income, occupancy and cancellations, sent to the host or owner on a date. Why: owners on the Philippine side like a regular written statement.
- **Should: Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR on the dashboard.** Use the standard definitions from Hostaway. Why: these are the first numbers a host checks each month.
- **Should: Tags for grouping units.** For example "Makati condos", "BGC", "Owner A". Why: lets a manager compare groups of units in one view.
- **Should: Bulk rate and block changes across selected units.** Set a holiday rate or block dates on many units at once. Why: saves time during Christmas and Holy Week price changes.
- **Could: Cleaning performance report.** Time from start to finish, late cleans and missed cleans per cleaner. Why: helps hosts decide whether to keep a cleaner.
- **Could: Owner portal with a login.** Owners see their own calendar and statements. Why: cuts the number of messages hosts answer about payouts.
- **Could: Shared live dashboard link.** A read-only link for an owner. Why: a simpler version of the owner portal.
- **Could: Team-only passwordless sign-in by email or SMS.** Why: staff who do not want another password.
- **Could: Lead-time and length-of-stay report.** Why: useful for pricing, but not urgent for small hosts.
- **Could: Team permissions by feature (not just role).** For example, a cleaner can see tasks but not prices. Why: finer control later, after roles work.
- **Could: Cleaning status on the calendar.** A unit shows "clean" or "not clean" for the next guest. Why: staff see readiness at a glance.

Not recommended for now: shift and on-call scheduling (no competitor does it well, and it is a large build for a small user base; revisit after the cleaner login is in use).

## 7. Excluded (integrations/API/AI)

- Channel-manager and calendar connections (Guesty and Hospitable channel sync).
- Accounting-software sync (QuickBooks, Hospitable and Guesty Accounting).
- Public API and MCP access (Hospitable owner API).
- Third-party cleaning apps and integrations (Hostaway with resort cleaning apps; Uplisting cleaners app as a separate product).
- AI reply suggestions (Lodgify, Guesty AI replies).
- AI assistants and automated AI messages in any product.
