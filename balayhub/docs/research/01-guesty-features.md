## 1. Summary

- Guesty is a property management platform for short-term rentals. It is based in Tel Aviv and was founded in 2013 (one source says it began under the name SuperHost).
- It is built for hosts and managers who run many listings across Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and their own direct site.
- Its core: one unified inbox, one multi-calendar, automated guest messages, task management for cleaners, owner statements and an owner portal.
- It sells add-ons: payments, pricing optimisation, ID verification, financing and accounting.
- Plans are split by listing count: Lite for 1–3 listings, Pro for 4–199, Enterprise for 200+.
- Pro and Enterprise prices are quote-only. Reviewers say this makes cost hard to forecast.
- Market position: strongest with operators who have 10+ listings across several channels. Weaker for a 1–3 unit host who wants simple, cheap, phone-based support.
- Scale: funding of about $170M in 2018 (Series E) and about $130M in 2024 (Series F). Valuation and total funding vary by source, so treat exact numbers as unverified.

## 4. Standout features

Ordered by how often reviewers praised them in the summaries we read.

1. **Unified inbox.** Guest messages from Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and direct bookings land in one place. Reviewers praise it most. Sources: [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/), [Capterra 2026 page](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/4699758/).
2. **Automated messages.** Messages fire at check-in, mid-stay and checkout. Reviewers say this saves a lot of time once set up. Sources: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/4699758/), [Ainora review](https://ainora.lt/blog/guesty-ai-review-alternatives-2026).
3. **Task workflows for cleaners.** One user says the tasks, cleaners and owner statements are powerful after setup. Source: [Ainora review](https://ainora.lt/blog/guesty-ai-review-alternatives-2026).
4. **Multi-calendar.** One view of bookings and availability across channels. Sources: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/4699758/), [The Host Daily](https://thehostdaily.com/guesty-review-2025-complete-analysis/).
5. **Automatic event pricing.** A G2 reviewer says the price tool detects local events and changes rates, and that it replaced a revenue manager. Source: [G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/guesty/reviews). Single reviewer; unverified.
6. **Owner portal.** Owners can see occupancy, revenue and statements without calling the host. Sources: [Guesty feature summary via search](https://www.g2.com/products/guesty/reviews), [Rental Scaling](https://rentalscaling.com/guesty-reviews/). Tier unclear.
7. **Scale for multi-channel operators.** Reviewers say it works best for operators with 10+ listings on several sites. Source: [Rental Scaling](https://rentalscaling.com/guesty-reviews/).
8. **Growth story.** One Capterra property manager says Guesty helped them grow from 30 listings to 150+. Single anecdote. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).
9. **Overall rating.** Capterra shows about 86% positive from roughly 460 reviews (figures from the search summary, not checked on the page). Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).

## 5. Complaints and gaps from reviews

**Price and billing**
- Reviewers call pricing high and unclear. Costs rise with add-ons and scale. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).
- One Lite user found the annual fee plus monthly charges steep for a small host. Guesty replied that it is reviewing its add-on structure. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).
- Pro and Enterprise are quote-only, which makes budgeting hard. Source: [Ainora review](https://ainora.lt/blog/guesty-ai-review-alternatives-2026).
- Reviewers mention hidden fees and billing confusion. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).

**Support**
- Support quality is inconsistent. One reviewer gave 1 out of 5 and said account problems take too long. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).
- Lite has no live agent. Help is by email only. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).
- Other reviewers praise support, so experience varies by region and plan. Source: [Ainora review](https://ainora.lt/blog/guesty-ai-review-alternatives-2026).

**Bugs and sync**
- Reviewers report sync errors with Vrbo and Booking.com, and occasional technical glitches. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/).

**Complexity and fit for small hosts**
- The platform has a steep learning curve. New staff can find it overwhelming. Sources: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/159377/Guesty/reviews/), [Host Paradise](https://hostersparadise.com/guesty-review/).
- Best fit is 10+ listings on several channels. Source: [Rental Scaling](https://rentalscaling.com/guesty-reviews/).

**Missing or weak features**
- Owner portal does not show tasks. Source: [Ainora review](https://ainora.lt/blog/guesty-ai-review-alternatives-2026) (quoting one reviewer).
- Reporting depth is weaker than Lodgify. Occupancy, RevPAR and ADR need export to a spreadsheet. Source: [Ainora review](https://ainora.lt/blog/guesty-ai-review-alternatives-2026). Partly contradicted 2026-10-13: Guesty's help center shows occupancy and ADR on screen; export is only in Advanced Analytics.
- Revenue features sit behind higher tiers, which some reviewers call a hidden cost. Source: [Ainora review](https://ainora.lt/blog/guesty-ai-review-alternatives-2026).

**Expectations**
- Several reviewers say it does not give fully passive income. It also removes some personal contact with guests. Source: [Awning review](https://awning.com/post/guesty-review).

## 6. Ideas for BalayHub

Each idea is built on BalayHub's current features. None uses an integration or AI.

- **Must: Owner read-only view.** Owners log in and see occupancy, income and their statements. Filipino owners who do not manage the unit want this, and it replaces "send me a screenshot" messages.
- **Must: Show a clear public price.** Publish plan prices and per-listing rates on the site. Guesty's hidden quotes are a top complaint, and a 1–10 unit host will compare costs first.
- **Must: Automatic cleaning task at checkout.** A cleaning job is created when a stay ends and sent to the assigned cleaner. This is the most-praised workflow. Check the code first, since it may already exist.
- **Should: Event-based rate rules.** Let hosts set higher prices for named dates such as Holy Week, long weekends, Christmas and New Year, local fiestas, or big concerts. Rule-based, so it is not an AI feature.
- **Should: Occupancy and average-nightly-rate reports.** Show occupancy and average rate per unit and per month. Hosts ask for these numbers and reviewers say Guesty makes them export.
- **Should: Taglish house manual per unit.** A guest page with Wi-Fi, parking, rules and building info, in English and Taglish. Cuts repeated questions.
- **Should: Cleaner schedule and job list on the phone.** A simple list of today's jobs for each cleaner, with the photo checklist. Many small hosts rely on one or two cleaners.
- **Should: Pay-by-link for extras.** Send a payment link for late checkout, an extra guest or a damage charge. Builds on the existing pay-the-balance link.
- **Could: Last-minute discount rule.** Lower the price for unsold nights in the next few days. Fills gaps and is easy to explain.
- **Could: Guest upsells.** Sell early check-in, late checkout or a welcome pack from the stay page.
- **Could: Linen and supply stock.** Track towels, sheets and toiletries per unit with a low-stock flag.
- **Could: Staff shift planner.** Show who is on duty for cleaning and check-in each day.
- **Could: Vendor assignment for repairs.** Assign a maintenance job to a named plumber or aircon technician and track the cost. Extends the existing monthly-rental maintenance.

## 7. Excluded (integrations/API/AI)

Left out on purpose, per the research brief. Do not recommend these.

- Channel-manager connections and two-way sync with Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Guesty's 60+ sites.
- The open or public API, and any integration marketplace.
- Smart-lock and third-party app connections (including Guesty LocksManager).
- Accounting-software sync.
- Guesty's AI features, including reply drafts and AI-branded pricing or messaging.
- Guesty's Verify and travel-protection products, where they depend on an outside provider.

### Guest app and guest experience (guidebook, upsells, check-in)
- Guest App is web-based. It pulls listing photos, amenities and house rules into a guide. Guesty suggests an automated message that sends the app link after booking. Source: https://guesty.com/blog/creating-a-digital-guidebook-for-your-short-term-rental
- Upsells: Guest App upsells are offered after booking. Booking Engine upsells are offered at checkout. Templates include early check-in or late check-out, welcome basket and baby crib. Guest App upsells are charged only after the property manager approves, and carry a monthly Guesty add-on fee. The help center labels the feature both "Beta" and "Pilot" (inconsistent). Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/38659478074909-Understanding-the-difference-between-Guest-App-Upsells-and-Booking-Engine-upsells ; https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/33469764321693-Beta-Increasing-revenue-by-offering-Guest-App-Upsells
- Optional AI agent offers early check-in or late check-out. Excluded (AI).
- Check-in: multiple check-in forms can be made, with dynamic variables in the terms. Source: same guidebook blog.
- Third-party option: Happy Guest marketplace app covers check-in, ID collection and guidebook, with no app for guests to download. Third party, not Guesty native. Source: https://www.guesty.com/marketplace-items/happy-guest
- Table correction made: guidebook row changed from "Unverified" to "No" for BalayHub.

- Cleaners can be added as users and grouped. Tasks can be assigned to them and they get notified when a property is ready. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20187928852637-Managing-your-cleaning-operations-in-Guesty

- Auto tasks: calendar-based tasks are created per reservation automatically; one-off tasks can be added by hand for long stays. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/sections/9879759538973-Auto-tasks-Calendar-based-tasks ; https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9385028548509-Best-practices-Tasks

- Task reports are an operator feature under Operations, then Field ops, then Tasks. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/sections/9112098852509-Managing-owner-statements-payouts (search snippet only for the path)

- Custom reservation report for owners: the operator sets columns (revenue, net income, cleaning-fee expenses). It is filtered per owner, and owners cannot change filters or columns. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/23505194583453-Setting-up-a-custom-reservation-report-for-owners

### GuestyPay, payments, security deposits and automation
- Payment automation under Guest payments. A "Security deposit" payment type can be added, as a fixed amount or a percentage, with its own timing. Deposits are then scheduled for all eligible reservations. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/31845297472797 (search snippet)
- Deposits are held and released automatically. To keep money for damage, staff must charge the deposit before the hold ends. Holds last at most five days, a processor limit. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/sections/19062675750941-Security-deposit ; https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/sections/17271831897629-Security-deposit-and-authorization-hold
- Deposits cannot go on a virtual card. Automated deposits sync to booking channels except Airbnb. Same security-deposit sections.
- The processor must support deposit holds. Help center names Stripe, Hyp and Merchant Warrior as alternatives to GuestyPay. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/31845297472797 (snippet)
- GuestyPay marketing page: card and payout features and fee percentages could not be read (page fetch failed). Source: https://www.guesty.com/features/guestypay (**unverified** for specifics)

### PriceOptimizer (rules only, not AI)
- Guesty describes PriceOptimizer as a machine-learning recommendation tool. Its rules layer is separate. Correction made to the feature table. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19327541350813-Understanding-pricing-and-pricing-tools-in-Guesty ; https://web2025dev.guesty.com/features/guesty-priceoptimizer
- Rules include base-price rules by holiday, season, weekday and last-minute strategy; price limits by year, season or date; minimum nights; and event-based pricing. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9359361787805-Guesty-PriceOptimizer-customization-features-Explained
- Minimum-night rules: by day of week, time period and upcoming availability. Gap nights shorter than the minimum can have their minimum and price lowered. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/15864878363293
- Rule priority: manual multi-calendar edits sit at the top. Later rules override earlier ones only for the parameters they set. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/15864878363293 (same article)
- Last-minute rules: a percentage of base price plus other factors, with conservative (20% lower) or aggressive (20% higher) presets, or steps by days before check-in. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/15888818227741
- Caution: minimum-night rules cause pricing mismatches if the listing is also on a third-party pricing tool. Source: minimum-night article above.

### Multi-unit and room-type support
- Property types: single-unit, multi-unit or complex, chosen at creation. The type cannot be changed afterwards (except a test property converted to single-unit). Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9364048715421-Creating-a-listing-single-unit-multi-unit-and-complex
- A multi-unit is a group of sub-units at one address, with the same bed arrangement and shared amenities, size and rates. Sub-units can be connected or auto-created. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/sections/19613359391261-Managing-sub-units-multi-units-and-complexes
- Room types describe sleeping arrangement. A listing can have several. Only needed for Booking.com. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/sections/19056056794525-Property-type-listing-type-room-type-room-arrangement
- Channel rule: multi-units push to Airbnb and Booking.com. Other channels need each sub-unit as a separate listing. Complexes link to Booking.com only. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19714851008669 (snippet)
- Airbnb: use Apartment, Condominium or Dorm for multi-units, to avoid sync problems. Source: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/33014642005277 (snippet)
