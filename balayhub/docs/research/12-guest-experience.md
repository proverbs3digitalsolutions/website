# 12. Guest experience features across the short-term rental market

Researched 2026-10-13. Products: Touch Stay, Hostfully Guidebooks, Enso Connect, Duve, Chekin, Autohost, SUPERHOG (now called Truvi), and Guesty's guest app.
Many sources are vendor marketing. Where a claim could not be confirmed, it is marked "unverified".

## 1. Summary

- Guest experience tools are the digital "welcome" side of a stay. They send the guest a guidebook, check-in form, house rules, Wi-Fi and entry details, and upsell offers.
- The leaders are Touch Stay, Hostfully, Enso Connect, Duve and Guesty's guest app. Most of them sell to hotels and to bigger vacation rental firms first.
- Common core: a no-download guest link, a guidebook, online check-in with ID upload, and paid add-ons.
- Upsells (early check-in, late checkout) are the big revenue claim. Vendors quote large per-guest numbers, but these are self-reported and not guaranteed.
- Pricing is mostly per property per year or per month, with some products taking a cut of upsell sales. Several vendors do not publish prices.
- None of the sources found name Filipino hosts as a target market. Most of these tools are priced in dollars and built for English-first teams.
- BalayHub already has the basics: ID upload at check-in, scheduled messages, PayMongo payment links, and Taglish for guests. The biggest gaps are a shareable guidebook, paid upsells, and a digital house-rules signature.

## 4. Standout features

1. **One guest link for everything.** Guests check in, read the guidebook, pay and send requests from one link with no download. Guesty and Duve both lead with this. (Guesty: https://www.guesty.com/features/guest-app/ ; Duve: https://duve.com/hotel-guest-app/ )
2. **Guidebook that updates everywhere at once.** Hostfully lets hosts clone guidebooks across properties and push shared content changes. Touch Stay creates a guidebook for each Guesty property automatically. (Hostfully blog: https://www.hostfully.com/blog/what-is-a-digital-guidebook/ ; Guesty marketplace: https://www.guesty.com/marketplace-items/touch-stay/ )
3. **Upsells sold in the guide.** Upsells appear in the guidebook at the right moment. Touch Stay has price settings and order alerts. Enso sends different upsells by trip type. Guesty changes the booking time automatically on early check-in or late checkout. (Touch Stay: https://touchstay.com/blog/best-welcome-book-for-vacation-rentals ; Enso: https://ensoconnect.com/features/guest-journey-editor ; Guesty help: https://help.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/33469764321693-Increasing-revenue-with-Guest-App-Upsells )
4. **Check-in that does the paperwork.** Guests upload ID, sign house rules and agreements, and pay before arrival. Entry details are held back until the steps are done. (Happy Guest: https://www.happyguest.com/compliance/rental-agreements ; Chekin: https://chekin.com/en/ )
5. **Automatic guest registration in some countries.** Chekin sends guest records to local authorities automatically in several countries. Useful for hosts with legal duties, but it is an external connection (see Excluded). (https://chekin.com/en/ )
6. **Fast check-in on site.** Staff can check in guests on a phone in under two minutes, according to a partner. (Chekin partner listing: https://store.apaleo.com/apps/chekin ) Unverified figure.
7. **Multilingual guest messages.** Hostfully auto-translates guidebooks into 16+ languages. (https://www.hostfully.com/digital-guidebooks/features/multi-language/ )
8. **Automatic review requests with private feedback step.** Timing and private feedback routing are common in hotel tools. (Hotel guide on review requests: https://chargeautomation.com/automate-hotel-review-requests-boost-ratings/ ; timing varies by guide, so treat as a guide, not a fact.)
9. **Repeat-guest direct-booking perks.** Vendors say a 10% returning-guest discount is common, and early access to peak dates is valued. (Houfy: https://www.houfy.com/blog/vacation-rental-repeat-guest-program-the-2026-guide )
10. **Wi-Fi capture for guest email.** Vendor claim: 85 to 92% of guests opt in when asked for email via Wi-Fi login, versus about 8% for a paper guestbook. Unverified vendor figure. (Houfy: https://www.houfy.com/blog/how-to-create-a-repeat-guest-loyalty-program )

## 5. Complaints and gaps from reviews

Most user review data is for the whole product, not for guest features only. Items below are from the sources found.

- **Setup is hard.** Duve reviewers say the most common complaint is setup complexity. (https://hoteltechreport.com/guest-experience/hotel-guest-apps/duve-app )
- **Limited design control.** One Duve reviewer wanted more control over layout and branding to match a resort. (same source)
- **Sync problems.** Buyers are warned about PMS and POS sync gaps and automation quirks. (same source)
- **Phone help is weaker than messaging.** A 2026 review says Duve's phone handling is weaker than its messaging hub. (https://voicetta.com/blog-md/duve-review-2026-guest-messaging-done-well-phone-calls-not-so-much )
- **Upsells can turn into mandatory fees.** On Guesty's booking engine, a fee sync setting turns optional upsells into mandatory charges. (HomeRunner guide: https://homerunner.io/docs/guesty-add-ons-upsells-setup-guide/ )
- **Guesty upsells only work on direct bookings.** A competitor's comparison says Guesty's native upsells are limited to direct bookings. (Hospitable: https://hospitable.com/compare/is-guesty-worth-it )
- **Guidebooks need a higher Guesty plan.** Adding guidebooks and payment info to the check-in form is only available on Guesty Pro. (Guesty help: https://help-lite.guesty.com/hc/en-gb/articles/24924676428445-Managing-Guest-App-check-in-forms )
- **Enso cost is unclear.** The price is only partly public, and a review reports a setup fee and a 5% cut on upsell sales. Reviewers say the fine print needs checking. (https://strspecialist.com/reviews/enso-connect-guest-experience-review )
- **Third-party screening is not a full answer.** About 3% of guests in one interview did not finish ID verification and needed another route. (Autohost-related review: https://www.bnbcalc.com/reviews/autohost-review )
- **Deposit rules differ by state.** One guide says common deposits are $250 to $1,000 or one to two nights of rent. State rules were not checked (unverified). (https://wonderguest.app/en/blog/furnished-seasonal-rental-agreement-free-template-2026 )
- **Damage cover is limited.** Guest-damage cover does not cover storms, wear or breakdowns, and contents cover is capped (one source: $150,000). (https://www.hostaway.com/vacation-rental-guide/vacation-rental-insurance/truvi/ )
- **Touch Stay pricing is confusing.** Multi-property prices differ across three sources. Verify directly. (https://guestintro.com/guestintro-vs-touch-stay )
- **Small-host fit.** Most reviews come from hotel or multi-property users. Small hosts with 1 to 10 units may pay for features they do not use. This is an inference, not a sourced complaint.

## 6. Ideas for BalayHub

Each idea uses BalayHub's existing parts where possible (guest check-in form, scheduled messages, PayMongo links).

1. **Must: Digital guidebook per unit.** One shareable page per property with Wi-Fi, house rules, check-out steps, parking, and maps. Reduces repeat questions from guests. Fits a host with 1 to 10 units who answers the same questions every week.
2. **Must: Timed Wi-Fi and door-code message.** A template that sends the code 48 hours before arrival, using the existing scheduled messages. Matches Airbnb's own timing and needs no lock integration.
3. **Should: Paid add-ons page.** Early check-in, late checkout, extra bed, and airport pickup, paid through the existing PayMongo link. Sold 7 to 14 days before arrival. Adds income for each stay.
4. **Should: House rules acceptance at check-in.** A timestamped tick-box on the check-in form, stored with the guest record. Gives the host proof that the guest saw the rules. Not legal advice.
5. **Should: Review request after checkout.** A scheduled message 24 hours after checkout with a review link. Pairs well with the existing guest reviews feature.
6. **Should: Private feedback before public review.** A short rating question sent before checkout. Low ratings go to the host privately. Protects the listing rating.
7. **Could: Returning-guest code.** Automatically issue a single discount code to repeat guests after checkout. Uses existing discount codes and repeat-guest tracking. Keep it to one code, not a points system.
8. **Could: Local recommendations list per property.** Host adds 5 to 10 places in English and Taglish. Keep it manual; no paid listings.
9. **Could: Translated guidebook.** Tagalog and Taglish first, then Japanese or Korean if guests need it. Keep translations human-checked.
10. **Could: Experiences affiliate links.** Earn a commission on tours booked from the guidebook. Needs a partner agreement, so it is not an integration with software, but check the brief's exclusions before building.

## 7. Excluded (integrations/API/AI)

- AI chatbots and AI guest replies: Touch Stay chatbot, Duve messaging AI, Guesty AI agent, Hostfully AI itinerary planner, Enso AI upsells.
- Biometric selfie matching and liveness checks (Autohost, Chekin): AI-based.
- Smart-lock integrations and automatic code sending from a lock (Airbnb smart-lock flow, Duve lock providers).
- Mobile key integrations (Duve).
- Automatic police or government guest registration (Chekin).
- Insurance and damage protection partners (SUPERHOG/Truvi) and their API connection.
- Guesty marketplace apps (Happy Guest, Touch Stay on Guesty) as integrations.
- Channel-manager connections (Airbnb, Booking.com, Hostfully, Guesty).
- Affiliate experience booking that needs a partner integration (Hostfully Viator, Enso Experiences). Listed as a possible idea only (item 10).
