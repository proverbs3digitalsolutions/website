Search budget ran out during this run, so Reddit and G2 threads were only partly covered.

## Summary

- Hospitable is a property management tool for short-term rental hosts, mainly Airbnb, Vrbo and Booking.com hosts.
- It is aimed at individual hosts and small teams with roughly 1 to 20 properties.
- Its main pitch is messaging automation, a unified inbox, calendar sync and easy setup.
- It also sells a direct booking website, with a basic tier and a paid premium tier.
- Reviewers say it is easier to learn than big enterprise tools. They also say support is weak and back-office depth is thin.
- Market position: the simple, low-cost option that small hosts move to from Guesty or from spreadsheets. Guesty is the bigger, more complex option for growing operators.

Sources: [Capterra pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/189280/smartbnb/pricing/), [Rakidzich 2026 pricing](https://www.rakidzich.com/articles/how-much-does-hospitable-cost-2026), [strspecialist comparison](https://strspecialist.com/compare/guesty-vs-hospitable), [Guesty comparison page](https://www.guesty.com/comparisons/guesty-vs-hospitable/)

**Direct booking add-on:** Sold as a subscription add-on on top of the base plan (one STR review).

**Direct booking fees (one source, unverified):**
- Direct Basic: about 1% host fee. The host handles tax, chargebacks and guest screening. Stripe fees come out of the payout.
- Direct Premium: about 3% host fee plus 4% guest fee, or a 7% host-only option. Built-in tax calculation, chargeback protection, fraud screening and damage cover up to $5M (US-only at launch, per the source).

**What drives the bill:** Price rises per property. Reviewers say the headline price climbs faster than expected once you add properties or smart devices.

## Standout features

1. **Automated guest messaging.** Reviewers call messaging the main reason to use it. It reduces the number of messages the host writes by hand. (Strong source: [Capterra summary via search](https://www.capterra.com/p/189280/smartbnb/reviews/), [TIDY summary](https://www.tidy.com/comparisons/hospitable/llms.txt))
2. **Easy to learn.** Several reviewers say it is simpler than enterprise tools. One Capterra reviewer said it is "extremely intuitive" for a non-technical user. ([Capterra review snippet](https://www.capterra.com/p/189280/smartbnb/reviews/))
3. **One inbox for every guest message.** Messages from each booking channel show in one place. ([Hospitable summary via comparison](https://hospitable.com/compare/guesty-alternative))
4. **Direct booking site with built-in payments.** Hosts can sell direct and take payment at checkout. The basic and premium tiers give the host a choice of how much work they handle. ([Hospitable direct booking page](https://hospitable.com/features/direct-booking))
5. **Dynamic pricing included in paid plans** (one 2026 review, unverified). This matters because most small-host tools charge extra for it. ([Rakidzich 2026 review snippet](https://www.rakidzich.com/articles/best-airbnb-property-management-software-2026))
6. **Low entry price and a free tier.** The Essentials tier has no subscription fee on Capterra's listing. ([Capterra pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/189280/smartbnb/pricing/))
7. **Flexible contracts.** The vendor says plan changes can be made any time, unlike Guesty's contracts (vendor claim). ([Hospitable comparison table via search](https://hospitable.com/compare/guesty-alternative))
8. **Calendar sync that prevents double bookings.** Reviewers praise it. This is an integration, so it is excluded from our ideas. ([Capterra review snippet](https://www.capterra.com/p/189280/smartbnb/reviews/))

Sources: as linked in each item above.

## Complaints and gaps from reviews

**Support (most repeated complaint)**
- "Customer support is hit-or-miss" in 2025 to 2026 reviews. A January 2026 Capterra reviewer said a ticket was closed during a holiday without being solved. ([Hospitable 2026 review aggregator snippet](https://thehoststack.com/hospitable-review-2026/), [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/189280/smartbnb/reviews/))
- Guesty offers phone and chat support, plus account managers for an extra fee, according to Hostaway's comparison (a competitor, so biased). Both vendors get support complaints in the RFP.wiki snippet. ([Hostaway comparison](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/hospitable-vs-guesty/), [RFP.wiki comparison](https://www.rfp.wiki/specialty-industries/hospitality-travel/hospitable/guesty))

**Price**
- The starting price rises with each property and each smart device added. ([Rakidzich 2026 pricing](https://www.rakidzich.com/articles/how-much-does-hospitable-cost-2026))
- Prices differ between sources (from $25 to $40 per property). Hosts cannot easily predict cost. ([RedAwning](https://www.redawning.com/pm/post/10-best-property-management-software-for-airbnb), [Rakidzich comparison](https://www.rakidzich.com/articles/best-airbnb-property-management-software-2026))

**Depth for bigger operators**
- Thin trust accounting, weak owner statements on cheap plans, and fewer integrations than Guesty or Hostaway. ([Review aggregator snippet](https://learn.10xbnb.com/hospitable-review/), [TIDY comparison](https://www.tidy.com/comparisons/hospitable/llms.txt))
- One Capterra reviewer reported a broken owner statement with no clear fix. ([Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/189280/smartbnb/reviews/))

**Bugs and small issues**
- Occasional bugs, including a message that failed to schedule after a review was submitted. ([Review aggregator snippet](https://thehoststack.com/hospitable-review-2026/))
- The direct booking website uses templates, so hosts get less design freedom. ([Review aggregator snippet](https://thehoststack.com/hospitable-review-2026/))

**Missing features**
- Does not answer phone calls. It only handles text and booking-platform inboxes. ([Review aggregator snippet](https://thehoststack.com/hospitable-review-2026/))
- No deep trust accounting. ([Review aggregator snippet](https://learn.10xbnb.com/hospitable-review/))

**Fit for small hosts**
- Good fit for hosts with 1 to 10 listings who mainly want messaging. Weaker for hosts who need financial reporting or many integrations. One aggregator rates it 4.4 out of 5 for that group. ([Review aggregator snippet](https://thehoststack.com/hospitable-review-2026/))
- Reddit: no Hospitable-specific threads were found in this search. **Unverified.** Search r/airbnb_hosts and r/shortTermRentals for "Hospitable" before relying on community sentiment.

**Ratings (note: aggregator figures)**
- Hospitable about 4.9 on G2 and 4.8 on Capterra. Guesty about 4.5 on G2 and 4.3 on Capterra. The same aggregator also notes support complaints for both. ([RFP.wiki comparison](https://www.rfp.wiki/specialty-industries/hospitality-travel/hospitable/guesty))

**Why small hosts choose Hospitable over Guesty**
- Guesty's own comparison says Hospitable is built for individual hosts and small operators. Guesty is built for growing professional managers. ([Guesty comparison page](https://www.guesty.com/comparisons/guesty-vs-hospitable/))
- A specialist comparison (July 2026) says Hospitable is the better fit for most hosts with 1 to 20 properties. It says Guesty wins once you pass about 10 to 15 listings or need enterprise features. ([strspecialist comparison](https://strspecialist.com/compare/guesty-vs-hospitable))
- Hospitable is simpler to set up and learn. Its price is lower for small portfolios. Contracts are flexible. ([Hospitable vs Guesty comparison](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/hospitable-vs-guesty/), [Guesty comparison page](https://www.guesty.com/comparisons/guesty-vs-hospitable/))
- Guesty has far more channel connections (60+ per Guesty) than Hospitable (11 per Guesty's comparison). This is a reason serious operators pick Guesty. It is excluded from our ideas. ([Guesty comparison page](https://www.guesty.com/comparisons/guesty-vs-hospitable/))

## Ideas for BalayHub

Each idea is for a Filipino host with 1 to 10 units. Excluded items (AI, channel connections, accounting sync) are not included here.

- **Must: Flat, visible per-unit price page in the app.** Hosts hate surprise price rises from added units. A simple price table with no hidden fees builds trust. (From: price complaints)
- **Must: Free starter tier for one unit.** Hosts with one condo unit are the largest group and price-sensitive. Hospitable's free tier is a draw. (From: Essentials free plan)
- **Must: Faster, plainer support paths.** Support is the top complaint. A help page with clear response times and a visible ticket status fixes most of it. (From: support complaints)
- **Should: Rule-based price suggestions.** Suggest holiday and season price changes from past bookings (for example Christmas, Holy Week, long weekends). This uses rules, not AI, and builds on existing season rates. (From: dynamic pricing mention)
- **Should: Full owner portal login.** Owners see their own statements and payouts without asking the host. This is the main gap in owner statements. (From: owner statement complaints)
- **Should: Automated message sequences.** Build check-in, mid-stay and check-out sequences on top of the current scheduled messages. Keeps guests informed with less host effort. (From: messaging praise)
- **Should: Direct booking discount rules by stay length.** Hospitable direct booking offers tiers. BalayHub already has long-stay discounts, so link them to the direct booking page. (From: direct booking features)
- **Could: Direct booking guest screening.** Check guest ID and past stays before accepting a direct booking. Partly covered by ID upload. (From: fraud screening feature)
- **Could: Owner trust ledger.** Track money held for each owner, with in and out lines. Fills the trust accounting gap. Must check Philippine rules first. (From: trust accounting complaints)
- **Could: Cleaner payment tracking.** Track amounts owed to each cleaner per job. (From: operations gap)
- **Could: Phone-call logging.** Hospitable does not answer calls. A simple log of guest calls, linked to the booking, fills a gap without any AI. (From: missing-features complaint)

Note: tax on direct bookings (built-in tax calculation) is **not recommended yet**. Philippine tax rules for short-stay rentals were not researched in this run.

## Excluded (integrations/API/AI)

- Channel connections: Guesty's 60+ channel connections, Hospitable's 11 channel connections, and direct Airbnb, Booking.com or Vrbo connections.
- Open or public API and integration marketplace.
- Smart-lock or third-party app integrations.
- Accounting-software sync.
- AI features: drafted replies, AI guest messaging, AI-powered pricing, and AI assistants.
